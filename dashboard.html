Privacy

Your bookmarks, notes, groups, and preferences are stored in Chrome on this device. Sutra does not send your library to Devnetra Consultancy or run analytics or ads.

Manage groups

Create, rename, or remove groups. Turn on Hide to remove a group and its bookmarks from the launchpad and search without deleting them. Turn it off to restore the group.

Wallpaper

Bring a little atmosphere to your workspace. Images are resized before saving to keep things light.

Import from Chrome

Copy your existing Chrome bookmarks into Sutra. Chrome folders become Sutra groups where possible. Existing Sutra bookmarks are kept, duplicate URLs are skipped, and Chrome's bookmarks are never changed.

Sutra reads your Chrome bookmark names, URLs, and folder names only after you click Import and confirm.

Backup & import

Export your bookmarks and preferences to JSON. Importing JSON adds bookmarks and groups without replacing your current library.

New groups are appended after existing groups. Duplicate URLs are skipped; current settings are kept.
